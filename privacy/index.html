Privacy Policy

AGT Publisher, the publishing tool of the Any Given Thing YouTube channel. Last updated: October 24, 2026.

AGT Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Any Given Thing YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own videos and to set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, translations and playlists.

It is not offered to the public. It does not collect, sell or share information about anyone else, and it does not use cookies or analytics.

The access it receives from Google is used only to manage the channel owner's own YouTube channel. The credentials are kept in the channel owner's private environment and are never published.

AGT Publisher uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. For questions, contact the channel through its YouTube page.
